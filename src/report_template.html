What changes if you weigh reach more

Each creator gets two scores. Fit asks whether they can make the kind of video that sells in your account. Reach is how many plays their paid posts get. How every creator scored

Fit is out of 100: a child presenting (40), English or Hinglish speech (20), kids aged 3 to 12 on camera (20), food or routine posts (20). Weights follow how strong each pattern is in the ads. Reach is out of 100 on a log scale from 10k to 3M expected plays for a paid post. Blocks are the reasons we wouldn't sign someone now, whatever their score.
